## About Me

Hi, I'm Arya.

I'm an engineer by profession and a lifelong learner by choice.


I don't like defining myself by a particular technology, domain, or title. Over time I've worked across data engineering, software development, analytics, automation, infrastructure, and systems.

I believe most skills are learnable. Curiosity, consistency, and humility matter far more than choosing the "right" specialization.

This website is my digital garden — a place where I document what I'm learning, building, reading, and exploring.

> If you can't explain something simply, you probably don't understand it well enough.

Writing is how I test my understanding. These notes aren't meant to showcase expertise. They're a way of making ideas clearer, both for myself and for anyone else who might find them useful.

## What I Do

I'm currently a **Data Engineer at Woodside Energy**, working with data ingestion, transformation, and analytics infrastructure.

My career has taken me through **software engineering, infrastructure automation, data engineering, analytics, and Generative AI**. Along the way, I've worked with technologies including Python, PySpark, Snowflake, dbt, Azure, Kestra, Power BI, and GitHub.

I'm more interested in understanding systems and solving problems than attaching myself to a particular technology.
