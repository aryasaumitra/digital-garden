## Ideas I Liked

### 1. Chronos vs Kairos view of Time

The Greeks had two words to describe time the first is **Chronos** which refers to quantity. The second being **Kairos** which refers to quality .

Chronos is the basis for all the productivity mantra sold in the self-help market. The core idea is we are born with a limited time and each time unit is fixed and how we use it defines our life. Every week, Every day we get 24 hours and making the most of it the way forward. Its a good one to get you out of bed and watch some motivating reels and get you through the week or a month but it ignores an aspect of time.

Emotions affect our perception of time. When we are happy time seem to pass quickly and when we are in rut or stressed time seems to dilate hard and it seems the clock seems to have stuck. This is were the Kairos view of time comes that not every week is same even though the number on the calendar says so. Emotional distruptions are part of human life and ignorning them is practically living like a zombie. There are weeks when you get so much stuff done when your emotions and energy are aligned and and there are weeks when we simply need to take our time. 

The goal isn't to extract the same amount of output from every unit of time. It's to recognize that different periods of life have different capacities, and respond accordingly

This is were the Kairos rituals come in place. The core idea we have magic windows (Flow State) in our day when we are most in the zone like early in the morning or in the night when everyone is asleep and there are activities we do that gets in the zone like listening to music or getting a cup of coffee or writing down the plan on a piece of paper, a distraction list. 

> Where is my magic window ? What deep congnitively demanding task I can fit in this window and avoid multitasking ? How can I extend the magic window

### 2. Plus Minus Next

A simple self reflection method

Every week 

1. Write down the wins and postive things that happened
2. Write down the roadblocks and failure
3. Write down your plan for next week based above and anything which you want to accomplish

---
### 3. Why we Procrastinate

When ever we procrastinate for the task in hand. We is should ask these 3 questions

> Is the task needed ? - Head - if no then redefine the strategy

> Is the task exciting ? - Heart - if no then how can we make it exciting

> Is the task doable ? - Hand - if no then ask for help



The feeling of Procrastinating is a messenger which gives a signal that one of 3 out of sync
