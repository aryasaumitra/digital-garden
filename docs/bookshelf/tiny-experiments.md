::: tip Dancing with Disruption
Navigating life's disruptions isn't about completely abandoning hope nor its about blind tenacity - Always can do positive attitude. It calls for in-between approach - an active embrace of life's unpredicability. The key is to culivate your own version of "maybe". Stick to your pact and just keep showing up being an agent of change in the world that keeps on changing.
:::
