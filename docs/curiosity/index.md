# Curiosity

Not everything I learn starts with a goal.

Sometimes an idea bothers me, a question keeps coming back, or I wonder whether something would actually work.

This is where I keep those things.

## Experiments

Things I want to try, build, measure, or test.

Some may become projects. Some may fail. Some may never get started.

That's okay.

## Questions

Questions I don't know the answer to yet.

Some are technical. Some are about systems, people, economics, science, or everyday life.

The point isn't necessarily to find an immediate answer. It's to keep the question alive long enough to explore it properly.
